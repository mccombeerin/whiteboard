What is Block Whiteboard?

Block Whiteboard is a Zoom App for early elementary tutoring sessions. It provides a shared interactive whiteboard where tutors can place letter blocks, word blocks, drop zones, and free text that students can interact with in real time.

Getting Started

For tutors:

  1. Start your Zoom meeting as host
  2. Open the Apps panel and launch Block Whiteboard
  3. Students will receive an automatic invite to open the app
  4. Use the toolbar at the bottom to add text blocks, drop zones, and free text
  5. Save your canvas layouts using the "Save Layout" button for future sessions

For students:

  1. Join the Zoom meeting
  2. Accept the Block Whiteboard invite when it appears
  3. Drag letter and word blocks into the drop zones on the canvas

Common Questions

Why can't I see the app in Zoom?
Make sure you are signed into a Zoom account. Guest users may need to be invited by the host.

Why is the canvas not syncing?
Check your internet connection. Both the tutor and student need an active connection for real time sync to work.

How do I save a layout for next time?
Click "Save Layout" in the toolbar, give it a name, and load it next session using "My Layouts".

How do I zoom in and out?
Use the + and − buttons in the toolbar, or scroll your mouse wheel over the canvas.

How do I delete a block or drop zone?
Hover over it and click the red × button that appears in the corner.
