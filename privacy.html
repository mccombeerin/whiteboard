Block Whiteboard — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026
Operated by: Erin McCombe

Overview

Block Whiteboard is a Zoom App designed for early elementary tutoring sessions. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

What We Collect

Session data: Block Whiteboard creates temporary session identifiers to sync the shared whiteboard between participants. These identifiers are randomly generated and are not linked to any personal information.

Canvas content: Text blocks, drop zones, and saved layouts created during a session are stored in our database (Supabase) so they can be synced in real time. Saved layouts are retained until the tutor deletes them.

Zoom user context: Block Whiteboard requests basic Zoom user context solely to determine app functionality. We do not store your Zoom user ID, name, or email address.

What We Do Not Collect

We do not collect names, email addresses, or contact information. We do not collect audio or video from your Zoom meeting. We do not use cookies for tracking. We do not collect any information from children directly. We do not sell or share any data with third parties for advertising purposes.

Children's Privacy

Block Whiteboard is designed to be used in tutoring sessions with children under the supervision of a tutor (adult). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. All session content is created and controlled by the tutor.

Data Storage

Canvas content and saved layouts are stored securely using Supabase. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

Data Retention

Saved layouts are stored until deleted by the tutor. Temporary session data is cleared when the session ends.
