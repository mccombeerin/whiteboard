Setup & Authorization

Adding the App

Block Whiteboard is a Zoom App that runs inside the Zoom desktop client during a meeting. Follow the steps below to add it to your account.

Prerequisites

  • Zoom desktop client installed (version 5.10 or later recommended)
  • A Zoom account (free or paid)
  • You must be 16 years of age or older to use this app

Step 1 — Find the app

  1. Go to the Zoom App Marketplace
  2. Search for Block Whiteboard
  3. Click on the app listing

Step 2 — Authorize the app

  1. Click Add on the app listing page
  2. You will be shown a permissions screen listing what the app can access
  3. Block Whiteboard only requests the zoomapp:inmeeting scope — this allows it to run inside a Zoom meeting. No meeting audio, video, or chat data is accessed.
  4. Click Allow to authorize
  5. You will be redirected back to Zoom

Step 3 — Age confirmation

  1. The first time you open Block Whiteboard in a meeting, an age confirmation screen will appear
  2. Check the box confirming you are 16 or older (or an educator using the app professionally)
  3. Click Continue to Block Whiteboard
For school or organisation accounts: your Zoom administrator may need to approve the app before you can add it. Ask your IT admin to enable Block Whiteboard in the Zoom admin portal.
Feature Guide

Using the App

Opening the app during a meeting

  1. Start or join a Zoom meeting
  2. Click the Apps icon in the Zoom toolbar (grid icon ✦)
  3. Find Block Whiteboard and click Open
  4. The whiteboard canvas will open in a side panel
As the host, opening the app sends an automatic invite to all participants so they can open it too with one tap.

Creating a text block

  1. Click + Text Block in the toolbar
  2. Choose a tab: Letters / Words for coloured tiles, or Free Text for plain text
  3. For Letters/Words: click a preset letter or word chip, or type your own in the input box
  4. For Free Text: type anything in the text area. Select characters and click Bold or Underline to format specific letters
  5. Choose a tile colour (Letters/Words only)
  6. Click Add Block — the block appears on the canvas

Working with drop zones

  1. Click + Drop Zone to place a dashed zone on the canvas
  2. Drag a text block near a drop zone — it will snap in automatically
  3. Drag the block back out to unsnap it
  4. Hover over a drop zone and click LOCK to fix it in place — locked zones turn orange and cannot be moved
  5. Click LOCK again to unlock
  6. Hover and click the red × to delete a drop zone

Saving and loading layouts

  1. Set up your canvas with blocks and drop zones
  2. Click Save Layout, enter a name, click Save
  3. In a future session, click My Layouts to see your saved layouts
  4. Click Load to clear the canvas and reload that layout
  5. Click the red × next to a layout to delete it permanently

Zooming the canvas

  • Use the + and − buttons in the toolbar
  • Scroll the mouse wheel over the canvas to zoom toward the mouse position
  • Pinch on a touchscreen to zoom
  • Click ⊙ to reset to 100%

Real-time collaboration

All changes sync instantly between the tutor and student. When either person moves a block, adds a drop zone, or clears the canvas, the other person sees it within 1–2 seconds. Both participants must have the app open in their own Zoom Apps panel for sync to work.

Disconnect & Deauthorization

Removing the App

Removing from your Zoom account

  1. Go to marketplace.zoom.us
  2. Click your profile icon (top right) → Manage
  3. Click Added Apps in the left sidebar
  4. Find Block Whiteboard and click Remove
  5. Confirm the removal

What happens when you remove the app

  • The app is removed from your Zoom account and will no longer appear in your Apps panel
  • Any saved layouts stored in the app's database are not automatically deleted — contact support if you would like your data deleted
  • Other participants in your meetings are not affected — they can still use the app if they have it installed
  • No Zoom account data, meeting data, or personal information is retained by the app after removal

Requesting data deletion

To request deletion of any saved layout data associated with your sessions, contact Erin McCombe at the support address below. We will respond within 30 days.

Troubleshooting

Troubleshooting

The app doesn't appear in my Zoom Apps panel

  • Make sure you are signed into your Zoom account (not joining as a guest)
  • Try restarting the Zoom desktop client
  • If on a school or work account, ask your administrator to enable the app

The canvas is not syncing between tutor and student

  • Both users must have the app open in their own Zoom Apps panel — Play Together / screen share is view-only
  • Check both internet connections are active
  • Try closing and reopening the app on both devices

I can't save or load layouts

  • This requires an internet connection to the app's database
  • Try closing and reopening the app
  • If the problem persists, contact support

The age confirmation screen doesn't appear

  • Try refreshing the app or reopening it in the Zoom Apps panel
